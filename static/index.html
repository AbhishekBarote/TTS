    Audio Intake Guidelines

    Fast Duplicate Detection: Real-time SHA-256 content hashing checks candidates against approved records in <50ms.
    Duration Threshold: Recordings must be at least 10 minutes (formatted as MM:SS or HH:MM:SS).
    Accent & Pronunciation: Clean Indian English speech with audible spoken Sanskrit terminology.
    3.5-Hour Speaker Cap: Maximum 12,600s total duration permitted per Speaker ID across the dataset.
    Licensing: Verify Standard YouTube license, CC-BY, or public domain authorization.
